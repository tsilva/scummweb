SIERRA FAMILY DEMONSTRATION CD-ROM VERSION 2.0 8/1/93

Although this demo was designed to be run from the menu system
provided on the CD, it is also a standalone runnable product.
Simply type DEMO from within this subdirectory.

This demo cannot be run directly from the CD-ROM, you will be
prompted to copy the files to your C: drive.  It requires
approximately 1.1 mbytes of space.

From within the game, click on the skull to display the exit
menu.

Enjoy.

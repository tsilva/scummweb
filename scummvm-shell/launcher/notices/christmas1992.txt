
            Sierra On-Line Christmas Card 1992


Installation Instructions:
Change the drive letter of your DOS prompt to the drive
you put the disk in and type: INSTALL and press [ENTER].

Loading Instructions:
From the directory you installed the Christmas Card to,
type: SIERRA and press [ENTER].

Quitting the Christmas Card:
To stop the card from playing, press [ESCAPE].

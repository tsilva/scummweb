About the christmas card:

it runs best on ibm's with an ega card. With a CGA card, try ctrl-r
to get from black and white to 4-color color. Also looks great on tandy
1000 computers.

f1 - help screen
f2 - sound on/off
f9 - restart
esc - pause
+/- volume (tandy systems only)

To run it, just enter AGI
It was prepared as a demo for in computer stores, hopefully your kids
will like it. Its a little commercial but not ridiculous.

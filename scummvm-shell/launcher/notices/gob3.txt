IN this playable demo of Goblins 3 you take on the role of one of the happy
chappies, Blount, trying to save yourself, and your parrot friend Chump, from a
sinking ship. When you first load the program from the MegaDisk you will need
to run INSTALL in the GOB3 directory. Then make sure you are in the \DEMOGOB3
directory and either type GOB3 to run the program or SETUP to configure it for
your system.

Before you can start playing you must wait until the introductory screens have
finished. Use the mouse to point at an object. To move to it, click the left
mouse button while clicking the right will bring up your inventory. To select
an item from this, click on it.

Tip: Point and click the left mouse button at the club to add it to your
inventory. Click the right mouse button twice to use it. Click on the crown and
point this at the screw which is holding up the hook.
